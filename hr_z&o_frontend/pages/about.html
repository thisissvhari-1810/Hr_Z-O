Our story

PeopleFlow was founded in 2021 by a team of HR leaders, engineers and ex-payroll consultants who had spent a decade watching brilliant HR teams drown in admin work. Spreadsheets, biometric exports, payroll Excel sheets, compliance challans — all manual, all error-prone, all stealing time from the people work that actually matters.

So we built the platform we wished we had: one product that covers the entire employee journey, from the day a candidate applies to the day they retire — and every PF, ESI, TDS, gratuity and bonus calculation in between.
