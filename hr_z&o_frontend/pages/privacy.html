Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 June 2026

PeopleFlow Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("PeopleFlow", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have. It applies to peopleflow.in and our mobile and web applications (collectively, the "Service").

1. Who is the data controller?

For visitors to our website and prospects, PeopleFlow is the data controller. For employee data that flows through the platform on behalf of a customer organisation, PeopleFlow acts as a data processor and the customer is the controller.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data — name, work email, phone number, company name, password (hashed).
  • Employee records (when uploaded by a customer) — name, contact details, identifiers like PAN / Aadhaar last 4 digits, salary, attendance and leave history.
  • Usage data — log files, device, browser, IP address, pages visited, features used.
  • Cookies — see section 7.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the Service (authentication, attendance, payroll, etc.).
  • To comply with statutory obligations (PF, ESI, TDS, gratuity filings).
  • To send service-related notifications and product updates.
  • To improve the Service through aggregated, anonymised analytics.
  • To respond to support requests and prevent abuse.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process personal data under one or more of the following: your consent, performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation (e.g. India's DPDP Act, 2023), or our legitimate business interests.

5. Where we store data

All customer data is stored in Amazon Web Services (AWS) Mumbai region (ap-south-1). Data does not leave India under normal operations. Sub-processors used for limited functions (e.g. transactional email, SMS) are listed in our Sub-processor List.

6. How long we keep data

Account data is kept for the lifetime of your account and 90 days after deletion. Employee records uploaded by a customer are retained according to the customer's contract and applicable statutory retention rules (typically 7 years for payroll records).

7. Cookies

We use first-party cookies for authentication and to remember preferences. Analytics cookies (Plausible / GA4) are only set with your consent. You can manage cookies from your browser settings.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and the GDPR (where applicable), you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or erase your personal data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Data portability — receive a copy in machine-readable form.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing).
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@peopleflow.in.

9. Security

We are ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Access is role-based and audited. See our Security page for more.

10. Children's data

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service evolves. Material changes will be notified via email and on this page at least 30 days before they take effect.

12. Contact

Questions? Reach our Data Protection Officer at dpo@peopleflow.in or write to: PeopleFlow Technologies Pvt. Ltd., #404, Brigade IRV Centre, Whitefield, Bengaluru 560066.
