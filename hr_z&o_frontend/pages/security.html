Trust & Security

Your people data, fiercely protected.

Employee data is the most sensitive data in your company. We treat it that way — with certifications, controls and engineering practice built around that promise.

🛡️

ISO 27001

Certified information security management system, audited annually.

🔒

SOC 2 Type II

Independently audited controls for security, availability and confidentiality.

🇮🇳

Hosted in India

AWS Mumbai region. Customer data does not leave Indian soil.

📜

DPDP Compliant

Built for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Application security

  • All traffic protected by TLS 1.2+; HSTS enforced on all subdomains.
  • Customer data encrypted at rest with AES-256.
  • OWASP Top 10 controls baked into our SDLC; quarterly external pentests.
  • Role-based access control with fine-grained permissions and full audit logs.
  • Optional SSO via SAML, Google, Microsoft; two-factor authentication available on all plans.

Infrastructure security

  • Multi-AZ deployment in AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1) with automated failover.
  • Encrypted RDS Postgres clusters with point-in-time recovery up to 30 days.
  • WAF + DDoS protection via AWS Shield.
  • All production access is via bastion, MFA-protected, and logged.

Operational security

  • Least-privilege access for all PeopleFlow staff; background-verified hires only.
  • Mandatory security training, refreshed quarterly.
  • Incident response plan with 24-hour customer notification SLA for confirmed breaches.
  • Vendor risk review for every sub-processor before onboarding.

Business continuity

  • RPO < 5 minutes, RTO < 1 hour for the primary service.
  • Daily encrypted backups, retained for 30 days; quarterly restore drills.
  • 99.9% monthly uptime target with SLA-backed credits for Growth and Enterprise.

Responsible disclosure

Found a vulnerability? Please email security@peopleflow.in with details. We acknowledge within 24 hours and aim to fix critical issues within 7 days. Reporters who follow responsible disclosure get public credit (and our gratitude).

Need our compliance pack?

SOC 2 report, ISO certificate, pentest summary, DPA and sub-processor list are available under NDA. Email trust@peopleflow.in.
